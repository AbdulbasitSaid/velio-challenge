# ActivityCard

The activity at a glance: photo, spots badge, title, date, place, price, host and who's going.

- **Provide:** `title`, `scene` (art stand-in until real photos), `spots`, `date` and `zone` ("Nairobi time"), `place` (venue name), `price`, `host` (public form), `going`, `community`; `lead` adds an inviter line above the title ("Tunde wants you on").
- The price always reads "{price} · pay the host on the day" (UX-1).
- `compact` is for lists, community pages and next steps: title plus one meta line.
- Children render at the bottom, for the CTA.
